BlurItOut

Upload a photo, drop a blur shape, drag it into place — all in your browser.

Upload an image to get started.

Tap or click on the image to place a blur shape. Drag inside a shape to move it, or drag its handles to resize it — the width/height sliders update to match, and vice versa. Adjust the blur amount any time; 0% leaves the image untouched, 100% is a flat, unrecoverable smear. Select a shape and press Delete, or use the button above, to remove it.
